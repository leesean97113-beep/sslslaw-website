Which courts hear cases from Petaling Jaya?

Smaller civil claims are usually heard in the Magistrates' and Sessions Courts in Petaling Jaya. High Court matters arising in Selangor are generally heard at the Shah Alam High Court. The right court depends on the amount claimed and the type of case.

Do I need to come to the office?

Many matters can begin by phone or email. Some documents, such as property transfer forms and statutory declarations, must be signed before a lawyer or a commissioner for oaths, so at least one visit is often needed. We will let you know.
