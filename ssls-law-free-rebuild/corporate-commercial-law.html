Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a shareholders’ agreement if my company already has a constitution?

A constitution sets out the company’s internal rules and is lodged with SSM. A shareholders’ agreement is a private contract between the shareholders and commonly deals with decision-making, funding, share transfers and exits. Many companies with more than one owner use both.

What is needed to incorporate a company in Malaysia?

Incorporation is done with SSM. You will generally need the proposed company name, details of the directors and shareholders, a registered office address and the share capital. A company secretary must be appointed within 30 days of incorporation.

Do commercial agreements need to be stamped?

Many agreements attract stamp duty under the Stamp Act 1949, and an unstamped document may generally not be admitted as evidence in court until the duty and any penalty are paid. We will advise whether and how a document should be stamped.
